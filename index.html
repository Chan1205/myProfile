About Me

I'm studying Computer Science at The University of Texas at Dallas and expect to graduate in May 2029. My coursework includes Data Structures and Algorithms, Computer Architecture, Machine Learning, and Embedded Systems. I enjoy building things and finding practical ways to use AI in my projects.

I've worked on web apps, AI projects, and games, including a game recommendation app and a 3D horror game. I also served as an interpreter and translator at the South Korea Defense Intelligence Command, where I was nominated to be a squad leader. During my service, I helped lead interpreter teams and built a personnel tracking system. That experience taught me a lot about communication, teamwork, and solving problems.

What I like most about programming is taking an idea and making it work. Whether I'm building a game or working on an AI project, I enjoy figuring things out, learning as I go, and finding ways to make it better.
